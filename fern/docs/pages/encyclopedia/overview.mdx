A working reference collection of rhetoric: classical and modern figures of
speech, concepts, movements, institutions, and biographies of major
rhetoricians — organized alphabetically from A to Z.
</Lead>

## How entries are organized

Entries are grouped into letter sections in the sidebar. Each page follows
one consistent template so you can scan and compare terms quickly:

- **Lead** — a one-paragraph definition of the term or profile of the figure.
- **Thematic sections** — the body, broken into short headed sections.
- **Examples and quotations** — primary-source quotations set off as blockquotes.
- **Sources** — the bibliography, citations cleaned up and normalized.
- **Provenance** — a collapsible note crediting the original contributor.

## Entry types

Each entry carries a <Badge intent="note">Figure of Speech</Badge> <Badge intent="info">Concept</Badge> <Badge intent="success">Rhetor</Badge> <Badge intent="launch">Field</Badge> <Badge intent="tip">Classical Work</Badge> badge indicating its category. Cross-references appear as pill cards under **See also** at the bottom of each entry.

## Editorial standard

Every page is rewritten in contemporary academic English, reorganized under
headings, and formatted with callouts, quotes, and lists. Factual content and
the original contributors' credits are preserved; the prose is restated.
